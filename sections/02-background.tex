\section{Background and Motivation}
\label{sec:background}

Define the concepts, notation, and assumptions required by the method. Prefer precise notation over informal descriptions when ambiguity would affect the argument. For example, let $x \in \mathcal{X}$ denote an input and let $f_{\theta}$ be a model parameterized by $\theta$.

The empirical risk on a dataset $\mathcal{D}$ can be written as
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}(\theta; \mathcal{D})
  = \frac{1}{|\mathcal{D}|}
    \sum_{(x,y) \in \mathcal{D}}
    \ell\!\left(f_{\theta}(x), y\right).
  \label{eq:empirical-risk}
\end{equation}
